## Krisha Organic

**Q: What is Krisha Organics?**
A: Krisha Organic is the premium organic food and healthy lifestyle brand of Anti Bikli Ventures Pvt. Ltd., committed to delivering 100% natural, chemical-free, and high-quality food products that promote a healthier future.

**Q: What products are available on Krisha Organic?**
A: The products available on Krisha Organic are:
- Spices & Seasonings
- Sauces & Condiments
- Dry Fruits & Nuts
- Pulses & Grains
- Flours
- Oils
- Peanut Butter
- Chawanprash
- Rice
- Ghee
- Jaggery & Sweeteners
- Pickles & Preserves
- Herbs & Superfoods
- Tea & Beverages
- Ready to Cook & Mixes
- And more

**Q: What are the key products of Krisha Organic?**
A: Our key products are spices and seasonings, sauces & condiments, dry fruits & nuts, pulses & grains, flours, oils, peanut butter, chawanprash, rice, ghee, jaggery & sweeteners, pickles & preserves, herbs & superfoods, tea & beverages, ready to cook & mixes, and more.

**Q: What makes Krisha Organics different from other food brands?**
A: We bring transparency along with quality. Every product will have:
- QR Traceability
- Farm Information
- Batch Details
- Quality Reports
- Farmer Story

**Q: How do you provide transparency?**
A: The label on each product consists of a QR code which, after scanning, provides information such as:
- Source Details
- Quality Details
- Manufacturing Info
- Manufacturing Unit Details
- Supply Chain Track
- All Certifications and Compliance Documents

**Q: What facilities does Krisha Organic provide?**
A: We facilitate the following:
- Shop Organic Products
- QR Traceability Scan
- Farm to Fork Journey
- Live Order Tracking
- Monthly Subscription
- Secure Payments
- AI Product Recommendations
- Loyalty & Rewards
- Export Orders
- FreshMart Now Integration
- Sanvora Global Import Export Integration
- 24x7 Customer Support

**Q: Where can I buy Krisha Organic products?**
A: You can buy our products directly through several online and offline channels:
- **Direct to Consumer (D2C):** Official website, mobile app, and direct sales.
- **E-commerce Platforms:** Amazon, Flipkart, BigBasket, Meesho, and more.
- **Modern Trade Stores:** Physical retail stores, supermarkets, and organic stores.
- **Distributors & Wholesalers:** Available through a PAN India distribution network.
- **Institutional Partners:** Supplied to businesses, hospitals, schools, gyms, and restaurants.
